**Difficulty 5/5 · Gate.** There is no current account-specific end-to-end proof for the advertised discovery → proposal → submission → reply workflow. This is a business-model dependency, not something a passing unit test resolves.

**Complete when:** Choose the supported launch channels; use approved APIs or explicit manual workflows. Make capabilities visible per account and prevent unsupported actions. Verify discovery and receipts with authorized canary accounts before advertising automation. Do not build additional browser functionality until this channel decision is settled.
