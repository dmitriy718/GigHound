## Evidence and limits

1. [Van Inwegen, Munyikwa and Horton, Algorithmic Writing Assistance on Jobseekers’ Resumes Increases Hires](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.08083), submitted January 19, 2023; abstract opened September 13, 2026. The authors report an online-labor-market field experiment with nearly half a million jobseekers and an 8% increase in hiring probability for the assisted group. This is evidence about writing assistance on résumés, **not** a comparison of proposal tones, a guarantee for GigHound, or an 8-percentage-point lift. Do not attribute a conversion uplift to our presets. NBER’s page returned 403; the author-submitted arXiv abstract supplied the primary evidence.
2. [Upwork, How To Create a Proposal On Upwork That Wins Jobs](https://www.upwork.com/resources/how-to-create-a-proposal-that-wins-jobs), June 24, 2026; opened September 13. Guidance favors a specific client need, relevant evidence, concise presentation and an actionable next step. These are platform editorial recommendations; they are not a randomized comparison or proof of any named tone.
3. [OpenAI, Prompt engineering](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/prompt-engineering), opened September 13. Used for separating instructions, examples and task context. Prompt compliance, voice resemblance and factual accuracy still require output evaluation; contract tests do not certify model behavior.

Freelancer’s support page was retrieved but its available body did not expose the searched recommendation; it is not used as additional proof. No Fiverr-specific causal tone experiment was established by this search. Do not generalize the résumé experiment into a marketplace-by-marketplace success claim.

## Product interpretation

The sixteen presets in `backend/app/writing_voice.py` are our design interpretations of useful communication approaches: Clear professional, Calm and decisive, Consultative partner, Technical precision, Analytical thinker, Evidence led, Outcome focused, Collaborative teammate, Steady problem solver, Approachable specialist, Engaged and energetic, Creative perspective, Executive brief, Plain-language guide, Thoughtful diagnostician and Measured formality. They are **research-informed, individually unvalidated options**, without invented win rates or rankings.

Tone changes presentation. Saved writing examples supply recognizable phrasing and cadence. The current job supplies the subject; owner-supplied supporting records supply claims. Missing evidence does not imply inability, and confident proposed approaches/explicit estimates are permitted. The user asked to allow modest unsupported claims; we recommended against inventing past credentials, results and availability and explained the distinction. No prohibition or user approval of that recommendation is invented here.

## Evaluation still required

Use a consented, de-identified representative job set and owner-approved writing samples. Grade factual support, job specificity, tone adherence, voice resemblance, length, actionability and cross-job copying. Compare revisions without letting an evaluator see outcome labels. For actual hiring evaluation, record the precise text/tone used at submission, exposures, replies, interviews, hires and time spent, with platform/service/price context. R18 must distinguish descriptive win rates from causal claims; preset comparisons need sufficient prospective samples and an appropriate experiment design. Do not manufacture statistics before outcomes exist.
